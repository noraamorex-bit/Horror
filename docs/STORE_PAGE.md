# Store page (paste into Creator Hub → your experience → Basic Settings)

**Title:** Home Alone 🏠 [HORROR]

**Description:**

🏠 Your parents are gone for the weekend. The fire is lit, the pizza is ordered, your friends are over. It's the coziest night of the year... until the lights go out.

Someone has been living in your attic for nineteen days. Tonight he comes down.

👻 Realistic co-op horror for 1–4 players (solo or with friends)
🍕 Answer the door to a very strange pizza guy and choose what you say
🔦 Hide in closets and under beds, hold your breath, sneak past him in the dark
🤝 Rescue captured friends and escape: call 911, reach the neighbor, or take the car
🔎 No ghosts, no monsters. Every creak has a real cause, and the ending shows you what really happened
🏆 Multiple endings to discover. How many can you find?

Works on mobile, PC and controller. Invite your friends and survive the night together!

**Genre:** Horror · **Max players:** 4 (game servers) · **Devices:** Phone, Tablet, Computer, Console
