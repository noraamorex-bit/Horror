Your parents are gone for the weekend. The fire is lit, the pizza is on its way and your friends are over. The coziest night of the year... until the lights go out.

Someone has been living in your attic for nineteen days. Tonight he comes down.

🔦 A scary story horror game for 1-4 players. Play solo or survive the night with friends
🙈 Hide and seek with a stalker: closets, wardrobes, under the bed. Hold your breath when he opens the door
🏃 He hunts by sound. If he sees you, RUN
🏠 Explore a whole house at night, from the basement to the attic
🍕 Answer the door to a very strange pizza guy
📞 Fix the phone line, call for help, reach the neighbor or escape in the car
🔎 No ghosts. Every creak has a real cause. Find the clues and learn who he is
🏆 8 endings to find, one of them secret

Jumpscares, stealth and survival. No gore.
Plays on phone, tablet, PC and console.
